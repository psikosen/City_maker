# Procedural City 2028 (Godot 4.7.1 Forward+ / Jolt Physics)

A deterministic **2304×2304m** (`18×18` chunks of `128m`, `5.31 km²`) 3D procedural metropolis built in pure **GDScript** and **GDShader** for Godot 4.7.1 (**Forward+** + **Jolt Physics**), featuring high-rise skyscrapers up to `24` floors with multi-plane recessed windows, curtain-wall spandrel slabs, vertical brise-soleil fins, historic brick iron fire escapes, dentil cornices, limestone quoins, residential dormers/shutters/gutters, rooftop mechanical louver screens, `640–960` buildings across **17 archetypes** including **coastal oceanfront `beach_house` homes** along a golden sandy South Coast beachfront with a walkable cedar boardwalk, walkable ocean fishing/observation piers, lifeguard towers, striped beach umbrellas, surfboard racks, and swaying coastal palms; a **~49m-wide navigable river** emptying into an endless Gerstner-wave coastal ocean with Beer-Lambert chromatic depth absorption, organic warped underwater caustics, and breaking onshore surf rollers; working multi-story building elevators, signage-matched multi-floor interiors, **4-lane arterial IDM/MOBIL traffic across 8 sculpted vehicle archetypes** (`620` vehicles + `14` river vessels), **1,040 procedurally scheduled daily-routine citizens** (`NPCScheduler` + `CrowdManager`), and a **24-hour Day/Night & 10-Preset Dynamic Weather System** with physical vertex-displaced **snow piling**, snow-to-slush/wetness melting physics, and OBB-excluded 3D rain & snowfall (**Milestone 10**).

## Key Systems

- **Expanded 2304×2304m Metropolis + ~49m River + Seamless Horizon Skirt (`CityWorld.generate_plan_only`, `TerrainBuilder.build_horizon_skirt`)**: Deterministically builds districts, guaranteed urban/riverfront parks (`DistrictGenerator.ensure_district_diversity`), graded `2304×2304m` terrain extended by a seamless low-draw-call rolling hills skirt to the outer horizon, multi-pier portal-arch river bridges, hierarchical named street corridors (`RoadEdge.street_name`), dual-sided full block subdivision (`RoadGenerator` + `ParcelGenerator`), and **16 building archetypes** including `hospital`, `school`, `municipal_hall`, `police_station`, `fire_station`, `church`, `skyscraper_tower` (`12–24` floors), `retail_anchor`, `apartment_midrise`, enlarged `modern_office` / `mixed_use_midrise` / `civic_hall` / `warehouse` (up to `33.0m` spans), `brick_shop`, `townhouse`, and `house_pitched`.
- **Architectural Facade Depth, Historic Grit & Diurnal Building Life (`BuildingBuilder`, `building_facade.gdshader`, `architectural_glass.gdshader`)**:
  - Multi-plane recessed window framing (deep jambs, sloped drip sills, stepped header lintels), heavy-to-light ground-floor stone/concrete water-table plinth bands, every-floor curtain-wall spandrel slabs & projecting aluminum brise-soleil fins on modern towers, louvered rooftop mechanical privacy screens & exhaust stacks, and `1050m` skyline silhouette visibility on tall landmarks (`floors >= 6`).
  - Historic brick craftsmanship: rear zig-zag black-iron fire escapes (`floors >= 3`), stepped limestone dentil parapet cornices, alternating limestone corner quoins, and stochastic exterior window AC units.
  - Residential suburban detail: eaves rain gutters + corner downspouts, paired front window shutters, gable attic louver vents, brick fireplace chimneys, and pitched central front dormers on larger 2-story `house_pitched` homes.
  - Shader vertical weathering gradients (ground splash AO, periodic under-sill drip streaks, macro aging) and **time-of-day coupled interior parallax lighting** (`time_of_day_hour` in `architectural_glass.gdshader` syncing shop hours, dusk office glow, and staggered residential bedtimes).
- **Multi-Lane IDM/MOBIL Traffic & Sculpted 8-Type Vehicle Fleet (`RoadGenerator`, `TrafficManager`)**:
  - True **2-lanes-per-direction arterial lane graph** (`lane_index`, `adjacent_lane_id`) aligned to painted dashed dividers, plus single-lane collectors and locals.
  - **8 sculpted procedural vehicle archetypes** (`620` pooled vehicles + `14` river vessels): fastback EV Sedans, Crossover SUVs, Compact Hatchbacks, Crew-Cab Pickups with open cargo beds, Fleet Delivery Vans, Low-Floor Electric Transit Buses, Checkered City Taxis with glowing roof beacons, and Police/Emergency Responders with alternating red/blue roof strobes — all with alloy wheel hubs, side mirrors, ground-contact shadow skirts, suspension pitch/roll, and blinking left/right/hazard turn indicators.
  - Real **Intelligent Driver Model (IDM)** longitudinal acceleration with weather-wetness headway/speed scaling, **MOBIL-inspired polite lane changing**, synchronized 3-aspect traffic signals with sun-visored lenses, unprotected left-turn gap acceptance, "don't block the box" exit clearance, deadlock watchdog, scheduled bus stop boarding at `transit_shelter` props, live **pedestrian crosswalk yielding**, and **24-hour diurnal rush-hour demand waves** (`24%` quiet night → `100%` morning/evening rush).
- **Procedural NPC Daily Scheduling & Connected Crosswalk Crowds (`NPCScheduler`, `CrowdManager`, `pedestrian_walk.gdshader`)**:
  - Builds a connected citywide `PedestrianGraph` unifying road sidewalks, corner turns, signalized & unsignalized crosswalks, park greenways, scenic loops, and creek footbridges, anchored directly to real building doorways (`home`, `office`, `school`, `hospital`, `retail`, `dining`, `industrial`, `civic`) and outdoor smart-objects (`transit_shelter`, `park_fountain`, `park_gazebo`, `cafe_seating`, `bench`, `sport_court`).
  - **1,040 deterministic citizens** across 7 roles (`Office`, `Retail`, `Student`, `Medic`, `Industrial`, `Resident/Jogger`, `Night Shift`) with role-authentic wardrobes, commuter backpacks, staggered 24-hour daily schedules (`sleep` → `commute`/`bus_wait` → `work`/`school` → `coffee`/`lunch` → `evening leisure` → `home`), cached bounded A* pathfinding, and instant time-scrub projection (`T` / `[` / `]` / `--time=`).
  - Traffic-signal crosswalk obedience (stopping with idle breathing/head-look animations until `WALK`), bidirectional crosswalk occupancy reporting so cars yield to crossing citizens, 8m spatial-hash crowd separation, and weather-reactive **procedural rain umbrellas** (`PedestrianUmbrellas` MultiMesh) + hurried walking speed during rain.
- **Hospitals, Schools, Churches, Municipal Landmarks & Architectural Relief (`BuildingBuilder`, `BuildingGenerator`)**:
  - Reserved civic landmarks (`hospital`: `MERCY GENERAL HOSPITAL` with ER drive-under portico canopy, glowing Red Cross emblem, and rooftop helipad; `school`: `CEDAR CREEK ACADEMY` with classical colonnade, facade clock face, rooftop cupola, and schoolyard basketball court; `municipal_hall`, `police_station`, `fire_station`, `PUBLIC LIBRARY`, `MAIN POST OFFICE`) plus limestone and brick neighborhood `church` buildings (`ST. LUKE'S CHAPEL`, `GRACE CATHEDRAL`, `TRINITY PARISH`).
  - Rooftop architectural silhouettes: wooden stave water towers on multi-story `historic_brick` / `brick_shop` buildings, cellular telecom array masts on tall `modern_office` towers, brick/stucco chimneys on pitched residential homes, facade rainwater sill weathering in `building_facade.gdshader`, classical limestone domes on `municipal_hall`, hose towers on `fire_station`, stained-glass `church` windows, corner pilasters, balconies, mechanical penthouses, and helipads.
  - Commercial signage catalog (`BuildingGenerator.SIGN_CATALOG`) plus hospital, school, and church catalogs rendered with dark anodized frames, glowing LED accent bezels, crisp unshaded `Label3D` brand titles & taglines, double-sided cantilevered sidewalk blade signs, and upper skyscraper crown signage.
- **Signage-to-Interior Venue Matching, Working Elevators & Parametric Furniture (`InteriorGenerator`, `InteractiveElevator`, `FurnitureGenerator`, `FurnitureBuilder`)**:
  - `BuildingBlueprint.venue_type` ties exterior signage directly to matching interior room functions and multi-part 3D domain equipment placed with canonical `+Z = front` orientation and 2D Separating Axis Theorem (SAT) rotated OBB checks (`obbs_overlap_2d`) against walls, doorways, vestibule lanes, stairwells, elevator shafts, window bays, and neighboring items.
  - Domain equipment includes hospital recovery beds (`hospital_bed`) & diagnostic suites, school classroom desks (`school_desk`) & study halls, church sanctuaries (`church_pew`, `altar_table`, `pulpit`), city hall courtrooms (`judge_bench`, `court_bench`), police stations (`holding_cell`, `safe_vault_cabinet`), fire stations (`fire_truck`, `workbench`), `library` stacks, `post_office` counters & lockers, gym gear, cafes, grocery markets, bookstores, clinics, restaurants, banks, tech offices & studios, galleries, cinemas, bike/hardware shops, EV showrooms, plus general furniture and indoor trash/recycle bins.
  - **Working Multi-Story Elevators (`InteractiveElevator`)**: Installed inside tall buildings (`floors >= 5` and `hospital` `floors >= 4`), featuring enclosed shafts, bi-parting safety landing doors on every floor, hall call stations, interior `UP +1` / `DN -1` / `TOP` / `LOBBY` cab controls (`[E]`), live digital floor indicators, and smooth `AnimatableBody3D` lift physics.
- **Park Walkways, Plazas, Fountains, Basketball Courts & Creek Footbridges (`ParkWalkwayGenerator`, `ParkWalkwayBuilder`, `PropBuilder`)**:
  - Continuous terrain-draped greenways (`greenway`), arched timber/steel/limestone **pedestrian creek footbridges** (`footbridge`) linking East and West greenways with walkable Jolt deck and guardrail colliders, paved plazas alternating gazebo pavilions and tiered animated stone fountains (`park_fountain` + `fountain_water.gdshader`), multi-sport **basketball courts** (`sport_court`) on schoolyards and park clearings, road-sidewalk connectors (`entrance`), and scenic loops (`loop`).
  - Enhanced creek water shader (`creek_water.gdshader`) with rippled micro-wavelets, teal/emerald depth absorption, Fresnel sky reflections, caustics, and raindrop rings.
- **Architectural Multi-Floor Interiors & Switchback Stairs**:
  - `InteriorGenerator` and `InteriorBuilder` build corner-flush stair shafts, unobstructed ground-floor lobbies (hiding exterior vestibule shell trim cleanly via `ExteriorModeRoot`), mid-landing guardrails, smooth sloped ramp colliders with flush-soffit stepped treads/nosings, vertical newel posts fitted under aluminum handrails, and distance-faded ceiling `OmniLight3D` lights.
- **Streetscape Micro-Realism & Urban Infrastructure (`RoadBuilder`, `EnvironmentGenerator` + `PropBuilder`)**:
  - Roadways include multi-lane arterial dashed dividers, crosswalk zebra stripes, inbound white stop bars, ADA truncated-dome yellow curb ramp tactile mats, cast-iron travel-lane manhole hatches, and flush metallic curb storm drain grates.
  - Sidewalk, cafe, and park props add outdoor bistro umbrella seating (`cafe_seating`), sidewalk A-frame menu chalkboard signs (`a_frame_sign`), signalized crosswalk push-button posts (`crosswalk_button`), parcel pickup locker kiosks (`parcel_locker`), granite-curbed street tree planting beds, pure-GDScript procedural bushes & flowers (`FloraGenerator`), stacked street-name signs, digital billboards, wayfinding kiosks, hydrants, planter boxes, benches, bike racks, mailboxes, utility boxes, transit shelters, trashcans, streetlights, signals, EV chargers, and MultiMesh botanical trees.
- **24-Hour Day/Night Cycle (`AtmosphereController`)**:
  - Custom `sky_atmosphere.gdshader` (`shader_type sky; render_mode use_half_res_pass`) with Rayleigh/Mie scattering, solar disk, phase-shaded cratered moon, twinkling optical starfield + Milky Way band, 16-step jittered volumetric cumulus + wispy cirrus clouds with silver lining, and thunderstorm sheet lightning.
  - Physical orbital `SunLight` and `MoonLight` (`DirectionalLight3D`) with mutual shadow exclusivity and `CameraAttributesPractical` auto-exposure.
  - Pooled shadowless `SpotLight3D` streetlamps (`STREETLIGHT_POOL_SIZE = 18` nearest heads around the camera) plus automatic dusk/night emissive scaling (`MaterialLibrary.set_time_of_day_emissives`) on streetlamps, signage panels, aviation beacons, street signs, parked EV headlights/taillights, and interior windows.
- **Coastal Beachfront, Beach Houses, Boardwalk & Walkable Ocean Piers (`TerrainGenerator`, `TerrainBuilder`, `ParcelGenerator`, `BuildingBuilder`, `EnvironmentGenerator`, `PropBuilder`)**:
  - Sculpted golden sandy South Coast (`OCEANFRONT BLVD` at `z ≈ 2022m` → coastal dune shelf → `72m` wide golden sand beach → pulsing dark reflective wet swash zone → sloping seabed & endless `3100m` southern ocean horizon).
  - Guaranteed **pastel coastal `beach_house` homes** (`26+` per seed) with walkable interiors, front street porches, teal shutters, elevated rear ocean-view cedar decks on heavy X-braced timber stilt pilings, white timber + glass railings, overhead slatted cedar pergolas, 2nd-floor ocean balconies, and rear wooden beach stairs descending to the sand.
  - Walkable raised cedar beach boardwalk (`beach_boardwalk_seg`), **1–2 walkable Ocean Fishing & Observation Piers** (`beach_pier`, `62–96m` out over breaking surf with seabed pilings, railings, pier lanterns, benches, and a T-head shade pavilion), elevated coastal **lifeguard towers** (`lifeguard_tower`), colorful striped **beach umbrellas + cedar sun loungers** (`beach_umbrella_set`), **surfboard racks** (`surfboard_rack`), and curved ringed **coastal palm trees** (`beach_palm`).
- **Upgraded Procedural Ocean & River Water Shaders (`ocean_water.gdshader`, `creek_water.gdshader`)**:
  - `ocean_water.gdshader`: 4-wave directional onshore Gerstner swell vertex displacement, emerald backlit wave-crest subsurface scattering, `DEPTH_TEXTURE` Beer-Lambert chromatic depth absorption (`turquoise` → `sapphire` → `deep navy`), depth-guarded `SCREEN_TEXTURE` planar refraction, FBM-warped RGB-dispersed underwater sand caustics, rhythmic breaking onshore surf foam rollers + lacy cellular foam + crest whitecaps + winter ice rim, Schlick Fresnel reflections, and GGX sun/moon glitter.
  - `creek_water.gdshader`: upgraded with 3D trochoidal river waves, prismatic RGB-dispersed Worley riverbed caustics, enhanced Beer-Lambert absorption, cellular shoreline/pier foam lacing, raindrop impact rings, and winter icy-slush bank rim.
- **10-Preset Dynamic Weather, Snow Piling & Snow-to-Slush Melting Physics (`AtmosphereController`, `snow_particles.gdshader`, `snow_flake.gdshader`)**:
  - **10 weather presets** (`clear`, `partly_cloudy`, `fog`, `overcast`, `drizzle`, `rain`, `thunderstorm`, `sleet`, `snow`, `blizzard`) driving cloud cover, coastal marine fog / blizzard whiteout density, wind gusts, rain wetness & puddle growth, gradual **snow piling** (`weather_snow_depth`), and **melting-to-slush & wetness** (`weather_slush` → `weather_wetness` + `weather_puddles`) when warming up!
  - Physical vertex-displaced snow accumulation on terrain (`terrain_triplanar.gdshader`) and pitched roofs (`roof_shingle.gdshader`), flat skyscraper roofs/sidewalks/boardwalks (`concrete_scored.gdshader`), dark wet salted tire tracks carved through snowy roads (`asphalt_road.gdshader`), building ledges/bases (`building_facade.gdshader`), frosted canopies (`foliage_wind.gdshader`, `grass_wind.gdshader`), and horizontal vehicle roofs (`car_paint.gdshader`).
  - Camera-following `GPUParticles3D` rain streaks and drifting/swirling 3D snowflakes with nearest-8 `BuildingBlueprint` OBB indoor exclusion, grounded Jolt-raycast rain splash rings, traffic traction slowdown & NPC umbrellas during snow/rain, and full persistence of `weather_snow_depth` / `weather_slush` in `F5`/`F9`.
- **Separated Persistence (`PersistenceManager`)**:
  - `F5` / `F9` saves and restores `world_seed`, open/closed doors, player pose, and `changed_world_state.atmosphere` (`time_of_day_hour`, `time_paused`, `weather_preset`, cloud/rain/wetness/puddle levels).
- **Performance, Lag-Spike Elimination & Interactive Quality Controls (`PerformanceController`, `CityWorld`, `InteriorBuilder`, `AtmosphereController`)**:
  - **Interactive Performance & Realism Panel (`F4`)**, quick preset cycle (`F2`: `Low` / `Medium` / `High` / `Ultra`), and **Adaptive Auto-FPS Governor (`F6`, target `30 / 45 / 60 FPS`)** with rolling frame-time EMA + anti-oscillation hysteresis controlling 3D render scale (`50%–100%`), FSR 2.2 / FSR 1.0 / Bilinear upscaling, sun shadow splits/distance, SSAO, wet-only SSR, bloom, reflection probes, VSync, chunk draw distance, and traffic/NPC crowd density.
  - **Stutter-free interior streaming**: offloads `InteriorGenerator.generate()` to `WorkerThreadPool`, streams tall skyscraper interiors (`floors > 3`) incrementally via `InteriorBuilder.begin_interior_shell` + `append_interior_floors` (`2` floors/tick in separate small Jolt `StaticBody3D` batches), eliminates synchronous `ReflectionProbe.free()` stalls, and uses a `64m` building spatial hash for `O(1)` proximity queries.
  - **City-wide culling & tick LOD**: distance-based chunk visibility culling across the `18×18` (`324`) chunks, extended `BoxOccluder3D` coverage on multi-story and wide 2-floor buildings, genuine wet-only SSR (`weather_wetness > 0.06` or `weather_puddles > 0.06`), distance-gated procedural bump normals in terrain/asphalt/facade shaders, staggered far-vehicle/far-pedestrian CPU tick LOD (`TrafficManager`/`CrowdManager`), 12-bucket staggered NPC schedule polling, spatial-hashed streetlamps, and `6 Hz` HUD telemetry refresh.

## Controls

| Input | Action |
| :--- | :--- |
| `W` `A` `S` `D` | Move / Strafe |
| Mouse / Left-Drag / Arrow Keys | Look / Turn |

| `F2` | Cycle Quality Preset (`Low` / `Medium` / `High` / `Ultra`) |

## CLI Flags & Validation

Launch directly or pass view/atmosphere/benchmark flags after `--`:

```bash
godot --path /Users/raymondgonzalez/Downloads/city_maker -- --time=18.2 --weather=partly_cloudy --cam=street
godot --path /Users/raymondgonzalez/Downloads/city_maker -- --cam=gym --time=13.5 --weather=clear
godot --path /Users/raymondgonzalez/Downloads/city_maker -- --benchmark
```

Supported `--cam=` presets:
- World, coastal & civic exterior views: `overview`, `beach`, `beach_house`, `pier`, `bridge`, `street`, `traffic`, `crosswalk`, `creek`, `park`, `church`, `city_hall`, `police`, `fire_station`, `hospital`, `school`, `footbridge`, `fountain`, `sport_court`, `horizon`, `interior`, `stair`, `elevator`.
- Venue & civic interior views: `church_inside`, `city_hall_inside`, `police_inside`, `fire_inside`, `hospital_inside`, `school_inside`, `library`, `post_office`, `gym`, `cafe`, `grocery`, `bookstore`, `clinic`, `restaurant`, `bank`, `cinema`, `gallery`, `bike_shop`, `ev_showroom`, `hardware`, `music_studio`, `tech_office`.

Benchmark & quality flags:
- `--quality=low|medium|high|ultra`, `--auto-fps=on|off`, `--target-fps=60`, `--render-scale=0.85`, `--fsr2=on|off`, `--vsync=on|off`, `--perf-menu=on`
- `--benchmark` (`--benchmark-frames=<N>`, default `240`) runs a fly-through performance pass reporting frame times and draw calls (verified on Apple M4 Max Metal: **High VSync** `avg_fps=59.04`, `p5_low_fps=55.38`, `max_ms=39.66`, `peak_draw_calls=821`, zero `>40ms` spikes; **High Uncapped** `avg_fps=88.61`, `avg_ms=11.29`, `peak_draw_calls=713`; **Low Uncapped** `avg_fps=209.71`, `avg_ms=4.77`, `p5_low_fps=75.13`).

Run the automated headless validation suite (`5,526,364` checks passed across `--seeds=5`, including multi-lane arterial symmetry, IDM/MOBIL traffic scaling, connected pedestrian/crosswalk graph, 24h citizen schedules, and rain umbrellas):
